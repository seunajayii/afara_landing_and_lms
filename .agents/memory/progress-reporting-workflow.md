---
name: Participant progress reporting
description: Agreed cohort progress model for evidence-based individual, pod, and collective reports
---

Progress reporting uses platform metrics, milestone evidence, and written mentor/facilitator feedback rather than formal numeric scores. Each participant has baseline, midpoint, and final reviews, with a versioned project baseline and milestone updates. Feedback is participant-facing by default, with separate internal notes.

**Why:** AFÁRÁ needs to judge meaningful growth across different businesses and cohorts without implying false precision or reducing mentor judgment to a score.

**How to apply:** Use five default areas—learning and application, project/business development, leadership and execution, financial/operational readiness, and collaboration/network engagement—while allowing cohort-specific evidence. Generate individual, pod, and anonymized cohort reports from the same evidence model, with in-app and print-ready output.
